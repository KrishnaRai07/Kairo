Building a truly responsive, zero-latency mobile app in 2026: Why we went 100% Offline-First with SQLite & React Native. 📱⚡

When designing Kairo, our non-negotiable rule was zero cloud dependency for personal data. We didn’t want users waiting on loading spinners or wondering who owns their daily habits.

Here is the engineering & design breakdown behind Kairo:

🏗️ Tech Architecture:
• Framework: React Native 0.88 + Expo SDK 58
• State & Database: Local SQLite engine (`expo-sqlite`) with custom transactional repository & zero ORM bloat
• Graphics & Trends: `react-native-svg` custom Dot-Line Bezier graph with glowing gradients and interactive touch scrubbers
• Audio Synthesis: Native text-to-speech engine with bilingual localized salutations (Hinglish/English/Hindi)
• Security: SHA-256 PBKDF2 credential hashing with 5-point case-insensitive security question recovery
• Test Coverage: 64 comprehensive unit tests verifying calendar boundaries, notification planners, and Flow algorithms

🧠 The Flow Algorithm:
Rather than binary completion, Kairo uses a weighted 4-pillar algorithmic score:
Flow Score = (Execution × 0.40) + (Resilience × 0.30) + (Consistency × 0.20) + (Pacing × 0.10)
Disruptions recovered through rescheduling or late completion are rewarded instead of penalized.

🎨 UI/UX Philosophy:
Obsidian Dark Mode, 60fps haptic feedback, expandable accordion dropdowns, and high-legibility typographic scales.
